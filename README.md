# Proxy Forge

Make your own Magic: The Gathering proxy cards in the browser. Pick any image, type the card text, and print.

**Use it here:** https://weclock-droid.github.io/proxy-forge/

## What it does

- **Any image as art.** Choose a file, drag it in, or paste it with Ctrl+V. Drag the card to move the art and scroll on it to zoom.
- **Card text with mana symbols.** Type `{T}`, `{W}`, `{U}`, `{B}`, `{R}`, `{G}`, `{C}`, `{X}`, `{3}` or hybrids like `{W/U}`. Text in (parentheses) turns italic like reminder text, and `*asterisks*` make anything italic.
- **Four looks:** full art with a fade, full art with a text panel, a classic frame in any color, or a **custom layout**.
- **Custom layout:** every piece of text is a layer you can drag, resize and rotate, with 20 fonts, outlines, shadows, 3D lettering, vertical text, and box or torn-strip backgrounds. Start from a ready-made design (comic cover, torn strips, poster, vertical title, handwritten) and change anything.
- **Mana symbol styles:** classic, flat, outline, solid color or symbol only, in any color, for the whole card or one layer.
- **Your own symbols:** upload an image for any symbol code. Replace a built-in one like `{B}`, or make a new one like `{HEART}`.
- **Set symbol and rarity:** common, uncommon, rare or mythic. Upload your own set symbol and it's colored by rarity automatically, or upload a separate image for each rarity.
- **One-click styles:** seventeen ready-made looks, including classic, borderless, comic, noir, pop art, risograph, worn paperback and pulp comic, each previewed with your own card.
- **Art filters:** black & white, sepia, duotone, halftone dots, color halftone (pop art), posterize and film grain, with color pairs to pick from.
- **Wear & aging:** four levels, from lightly used to falling apart, so a card looks like a well-read paperback or an old comic: yellowed paper, rubbed-off ink, a cracked spine, creases, chipped edges, frayed corners, tears, a folded corner, crinkled laminate and old price-sticker residue. Shuffle damage for a different pattern.
- **Stickers:** put any text on a paper sticker, a barcode sticker or a crossed-out "sale price" sticker.
- **42 free fonts** from Google Fonts, grouped into classic, fantasy, horror, display, comic, sci-fi & retro, and handwritten.
- **Remix for ideas:** one click gives you a random real card, public-domain art picked to match it (from Wikimedia Commons, credited on the page), and a random look. Roll just the text, the art or the look, and lock the parts you want to keep. The first time you open the page, you get a fresh remix.
- **Look up real cards:** type a card name and the name, cost, type, rules, flavor, power/toughness, rarity and collector line are filled in from [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com). Pick a printing or the back side, and optionally use the card's official art.
- **Deck lists:** paste a list like `4 Lightning Bolt` (Arena and MTGO exports work too) and every card is added to the print sheet with its number of copies.
- **Sharpness meter** that tells you whether your image is big enough to print well.
- **Print sheets:** add cards (or card images you already have), set copies, and save a PDF with 9 cards per page at real card size (2.5 × 3.5″), with cut marks. Print at 100% / "Actual size".
- **Single cards:** save a 600 dpi PNG, with optional ⅛″ bleed for print shops.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and your print sheet is saved in your own browser. Card lookups and deck lists send only the card names to Scryfall, and Remix asks Scryfall for a random card and Wikimedia Commons for art.

## Run it offline

Download `proxy-forge.html` and open it in your browser. It needs an internet connection for the fonts and for building PDFs.
