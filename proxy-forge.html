    How it works

    Proxy Forge makes proxies: stand-in cards for Magic: The Gathering. People use them to try out a deck before buying the real cards, to play casual games with friends, or just to see a favorite card with different art. Everything happens on this page, in your browser.

    1. Pick a card. Type a real card's name under Card text and press Fill in, or type your own made-up card. Got a whole deck? Use Add a deck list… at the bottom.
    2. Pick the art. Use your own picture (Choose image…, drag one in, or paste), search old public-domain paintings and photos, or let Remix pick something. Drag the card to move the art; scroll or pinch to zoom.
    3. Pick a look. Click any style at the top, like Comic cover, Pixel hero or Tarot card. Fine-tune the frame, fonts, filters and wear under Look. In a custom layout, click any text on the card to move or restyle it.
    4. Print it. Press Add to print sheet, set how many copies, then Save PDF. Print at 100% (“Actual size”), cut along the marks, and slip each card into a sleeve in front of a basic land or an old card.
    Just want to play around?

    Press Remix to get a random card, art and look. Lock the parts you like with the padlocks, or open ✦ Your card and enter your birthday to get “your” card for the day.

    Is this allowed?

    Proxies are widely used for casual play and playtesting. They aren't allowed in official tournaments, and you shouldn't sell them. Proxy Forge is an unofficial fan tool, not made by or connected to Wizards of the Coast.

    Where do the card details and art come from?

    Card text comes from Scryfall, a free card database. Search art comes from museums and archives (Wikimedia Commons, the Wellcome Collection, the Rijksmuseum, NASA) and is public domain or free to use; each pick is credited under the art controls. You can also use your own pictures.

    Is anything uploaded?

    No. Your pictures, cards and print sheet stay in your browser on this device. Only card names (to Scryfall) and the words you search for art (to the source you picked) are sent anywhere.

    How do I get them the right size?

    The PDF puts nine cards on a page at real card size (2.5 × 3.5 inches). In the print dialog choose 100% or “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”. Card stock or regular paper slipped into a sleeve both work.

    Can I save a look I made?

    Yes. Under the styles, type a name and press Save style. It shows up under My styles, and you can export your styles to a file to share them.
